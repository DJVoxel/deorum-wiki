## Abilities
### Strengths
- Creatures have a soul value, and upon killing the creature, you will gain this value in soul points.
  - Upon reaching 50 soul points, the respective points can be assigned to magical [[Stats|stats]], physical [[Stats|stats]], existing [[Skills|skills]], or to gain new [[Skills|skills]]/knowledge that you did not have prior
  - Grafting a part (i.e. a limb) of a higher soul value creature onto a lower soul value creature will increase the soul value of that creature to some point between the two values. The value can be calculated as (\[lower soul value\] + \[higher soul value\] * \[percentage of body mass used in graft\])/2
    - This can be used to gain additional soul points because you've already gained the full value of the higher point creature from killing it, but the lower point creature gains additional points.
### Weaknesses
- Must assign these points during a "time of calm"
- Getting too many points in a short period of time can damage your soul point counter
- Outside of using soul points, learns all [[Stats]] and [[Skills]] 50% slower
