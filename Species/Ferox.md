## Abilities
- Starts with all of their physical and sensory stats at 50
- Learns a weapon of choice 50% faster
- Starts with magnetism magic at 1000 and metal at 500.
- Learns Magnetism 100% faster and Metal 50% faster
- Immune to Electric magic and resistant to all magics with Electric in them by 50%
- Can heal with light and dark
- Potions that have no metallic elements within them are 50% less effective
- Cannot use non-metal weapons and armour
- Weak to Water, Ice and weathering-based magic by 100%
- Weak to Charm and Possession by 50%
- Cannot heal with Nature magic and cannot learn Nature magic

## Appearance
- Has sharp wings on their back attached to the shoulder blades, made of metal, that have any metallic colour, and ranging from 2 - 3 meters long. Eyes are a metallic colour of choice as well. Bones are made of the same colour metal as wings. Blood is green or blue.

## Trivia
- The child of a Magnes and an Arch Angelion turned out to be a Ferox.
- They may sometimes start knowing the angel language
- For combat styles Ferox usually stay in the air and use their blades to slice up any threats as fast as possible. If their enemy has magnetisable gear then they’ll use that to immobilize them or backfire and hurt the holder. To defend their wings and body from physical attacks they’ll use metal magic to create walls/shields
