## Abilities
- Dexterity starts at 50 and goes up 100% faster
- Starts with a black laser pistol and a glaive (can use a Glaive in 1 hand (better with shadow based weapons (can shadow on birth))
- All other physical abilities go up 20% slower
- They are weak to light based weapons

## Trivia
- For their personality, Shadez are a fun race who love to joke around, usually scaring people from the shadows. Ironically they can also be very pessimistic at times. If they find someone who pisses them off, at first, they will often challenge them to an honorable duel, if the offender declines then Shadez will try to pummel them to the ground. In society, Shadez use their shadowing abilities for various practical purposes. They can navigate through dark and concealed areas with ease, which often makes them valuable messengers and scouts. Their ability to move silently in the shadows has also earned them a reputation for being skilled spies and infiltrators
