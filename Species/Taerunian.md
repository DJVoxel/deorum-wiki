### Strengths
- Takes 50% less damage from all sources
- Can be 100% resistant to all damage for a period of time
- Immune to debuff effects
- Can stack and hold the damage taken to let it burst out in all directions as an explosion upon hitting a threshold or holding it for a period of time
- Has resistance in each magic equal to (Magic Stat/10%)

### Weaknesses

- Immune to buffing magic

- Can only use utility armour
- Cannot use damage absorbing/resistant jewelry
