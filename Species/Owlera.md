## Abilities
- Starts with lvl 25 sniper skills and learns ranged weapons 50% faster
- Can go invisible but over time it slowly drains stamina
- Ranged and Projectile attacks do 30% less damage to them
- Sight starts at 50 with innate nightvision
- Wind start at 50 and learn 50% faster
- Melee attacks do 50% more damage to them
- Cannot use 2 handed melee weapons
- Learns how to use melee weapons 50% slower

## Appearance
- Have owl eyes (yellow, orange or white with a black iris and pupil), and have grey or brown feathers on their back. Their legs are equipped with talons. Their head is able to swivel 360 degrees.

## Trivia
- Owlera were once Harpys with defects leading to no wings. They migrated to forest and ground civilisations from their once inhabited skylands, to escape their inferiority compared to beings of the air. It is not entirely clear what made them evolve to lose their wings. Some believe it a mistake of the deity which created them. They adapted their unique looks, invisibility and gun skills from centuries of living in the woods as a means to hunt and hide from the sky.
- Sometimes start with Harpy and/or Owlera (variation of Harpy) languages.
- Owlera heat season takes place once a year and lasts a few weeks. The majority of this species, especially those in temperate or subarctic climates will mate in the spring. However, some Owlera mate during the winter months. An Owlera will invisibly stalk whoever they like, to find out if they are loved back. They are known for leaving around hints and presents for the target.
