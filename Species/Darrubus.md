## Appearance
Has no neck, knees or elbows. All areas "cut off" have blue flames emenating from them, with a golden edge at the peaks. Detached limbs can move freely. Has a pair of blue and gold succubi wings and a gold tipped succubus tail.

## Abilities
### Strengths
- Can see and talk to spirits
- Can see the soul-auras of other people
- Its flames inflict [[Charm]] damage over time, dealing triple damage to [[Spiriting|spirited]]/[[Ghosting/ghosted]] opponents
- Able to percieve the environment from its flames
- Can drain soul energy, increasing or decreasing a magical stat temporarily

### Weaknesses
- Cannot learn any magics other than those it starts with
- For every 1 meter a body part is away from the torso, it takes 1% more damage
- Needs to feed off of souls and spirits via draining/exorcising
- If a flame is struck with enough force it might go out, temporarily increasing damage recieved by that part of the body by 5x
