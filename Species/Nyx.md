## Abilities
- Upon using a magic, develops its resistances.
- Physical hits after using a magic also deal the magical damage without using the magic.
- Can naturally produce the magic in the form of a movement and general enhancement depending on the magic
- Upon using a magic, also develops its weaknesses.
- Using different magics and swapping uses up stamina.

## Appearance
- Bright green eyes, red thin scales all along legs, blood is white. Colours can change based on magic use.

## Trivia
- The Goddess of The Underworld created them to assist in her paperwork and other boring tasks but over time she released them as a reward for their help and loyalty.
- Can start with Nyxus (Underworld language found in the goddesses’ temples and havens)
- Nyx's fightingstyle varies greatly, however, they are going to change which magic is being used less often than other species, thus, they tend to focus more on high damage or high versatility magics more. Nyx will also consider using potions and equipment to form magic instead of themselves to make up for their ability.
- Nyx are known for their sporadic nature, often displaying impulsive behaviors and sudden shifts in focus. Despite their apparent unpredictability, Nyx possess highly calculative minds. They are skilled at analyzing situations from various angles, seeing the bigger picture, and making decisions that may seem impulsive but are actually the result of careful consideration. Nyx also love to obsess about random things, from treasures, to mundane baubles, to information to art...each Nyx's obsession you would find would be unique to the other. 
Having been granted freedom from their initial servitude, Nyx value their autonomy and independence. They are explorers at heart, seeking out new experiences and opportunities to further feed their insatiable curiosities. This exploratory nature has led to the establishment of Nyx enclaves in various parts of the world, where they continue to pursue their passions.
