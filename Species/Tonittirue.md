## Abilities
### Strengths
- Can discharge all their energy/magic at once, shocking all creatures within 5 meters
  - Anyone who looks directly at the discharge is temporarily blinded
  - After discharging, Light and Electric stats are increased by 25% for 24 hours

### Weaknesses
- After discharging, physical stats are halved for 24 hours
- If not discharging regularly, will enter an overcharge state for a few minutes, followed by an fatigued state for a week
  - If struck by lightning or hit with too much electric damage, they will also overcharge
- Takes 50% more damage from [[Dark]] and [[Earth]]
