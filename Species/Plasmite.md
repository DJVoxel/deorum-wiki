## Abilities
- Physical attacks do 150% more damage
- All harmful and negative magic do 20% less to them
- Learns fire, wind, electrical and plasma magic 100% faster
- Starts with 10 plasma
- Learns to use most weapons 50% slower (unless weapon is radioactive or electric conducting)
- Learns all but fire, wind, electrical and plasma magic 50% slower

## Appearance
- Eyes glow a faint blue, their hands pulsate whenever they use Plasma magic and their nails are a dark purple or blue.

## Trivia
- Two high level plasma mages got together, their child was a Plasmite.
- In combat a Plasmite will get as far away as possible (up to 5000m) and snipe the person they want to kill with Plasma magic. If they are forced to fight at close range and cannot teleport away, they will use their Wind and Fire magic to avoid all attacks while raining down Electricity, Fire and Plasma spells on the attacker. Most of the weapons they wield are also radioactive or electrical in some way so try to avoid being hit or it may be fatal.
- None, however during solar storms their sexual reproductive systems go haywire which can lead to them acting uncontrollably horny and behaving very different to normal.
