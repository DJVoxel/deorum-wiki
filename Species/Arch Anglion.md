## Abilities
### Strengths
- Has Wings
- Combined total of 500 [[Dark]] and [[Light]] and Learns both 50% Faster
  - Can form weapons out of both magics
  - Can freely move points between [[Dark]] and [[Light]]
  - Can [[Dark#Movement (Shadowing)|Shadow]] and [[Light#Movement|Teleport (Light)]] at start
- Starts with 20 in all weapon [[Skills]]
- Starts with 150 in [[Stats|Strength, Reflex, or Agility]].
### Weaknesses
- Learns all other magics 100% slower
- Is weak to [[Dark]] or [[Light]] based on current distribution (ex. 60% in Light, 40% in Dark means 40% Weak to Light and 60% Weak to Dark)
- Unmodified [[Stats#Physcial Stats|Physical Stats]] start at 10 on start
