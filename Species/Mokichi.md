## Abilities
### Strengths
- Creates an internal honey with positive/negative effects based on diet and precieved specialness of ingredients
- Can use Telekinesis to control honey's physical and magical properties. When doing so, Telekinesis counts as a species trait

### Weaknesses
- Takes +25% damage from single-target attacks
- Takes +50% increased damage from [[Wind]] magic
- If their stinger is broken off, they bleed extremely fast, and any honey primed in that location might leak out
