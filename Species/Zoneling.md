## Abilities
### Strengths
- Can become Agitated
  - Halve Magic stats
  - Distribute the removed stats across Physical Stats equally
  - Gain an aura where everyone around them has their [[Skills]] are reduced by 20%
- Can become Enraged
  - Same as Agitated but reduce Magic to 0 and distribute the additional points
  - Become half-ghosted
  - Develops claw-like hands and tentacle-like growths

### Weaknesses
- Allies are also affected by the aura
- Increased chance of mental disorders
- For every 5 points taken off a magic stat, takes 1% extra magical damage
- For every 25 points taken off a magic stat, takes 1% extra physical damage
- Unable to use skills while Enraged
- Enraged state is somewhat uncontrollable
- For every 30 mins in the Enraged state, -10% Combat Skills.
  - These points are distributed across the Physical Stats for the duration of the rage, but are lost afterwards.
