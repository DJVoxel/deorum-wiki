## Appearance
Bullian – Have huge white or yellow horns on their head that are spiky and sometimes curled a bit like a corkscrew. Have hooves/hoove-like material bottom of feet. Average height is about 2 metres.
## Personality
Male Bullian are normally very nice and will help others a lot, they typically like building things and manual labor jobs, but don't like to be told what to do when already helping. Females are very solitary and will only help if they get something out of it, but are more open to instructions. 
## Abilities
### Strengths
- When charging, agility is multiplied by 4, and the effect of magic on you is reduced. (Scales with Agility/Age mostly)
- 25 agility and 50 strength at birth. Learns strength 50% faster
### Weaknesses
- Hitting something from the environment with a charge is likely to KO
- Takes 50% more damage from electricity
