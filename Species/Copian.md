## Abilities
### Strengths
- Has 25 [[Stats#Bodily Stats|Strength]] at birth
- Has 30 [[Stats#Bodily Stats|Agility]] at birth
- Has 100 [[Stats#Bodily Stats|Reflexes]] at birth
- Can make copies of their body. When making a copy, all stats are lowered by 20% for both the progenitor and copy.
- Has no "original" body. All bodies share a consciousness
### Weaknesses
- Cannot learn magic
- Can only use light and utility armour
- Takes 20% longer to regenerate/heal if a copy is killed
