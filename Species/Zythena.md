## Abilities
### Strengths
- When "plugged in", all Physical Stats increase 50% faster
- When "Fully Charged", learns everything 100% faster

### Weaknesses
- When below 50% charge, learns everything 25% more slowly
- When below 25% charge, learns everything 50% more slowly
- When below 10% charge, learns everything 100% more slowly and stats are reduced by 50%
- If charge hits 0%, they will enter a coma-like state until plugged in.
- Takes 50% more damage from [[Earth]] and [[Fire]] magic
