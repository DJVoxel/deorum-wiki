## Abilities
### Strengths
- Wings and tail produce a dust that causes drowziness
- Dust can be used by the revcubus to teleport
- When asleep, can still train magics and can enter others' dreams
- Mist coming from horns causes inebriation when inhaled

### Weaknesses
- Takes 50% more damage from [[Light]], [[Dark]], and [[Balance]]
- Must go into others' dreams to survive / feed
- Easier to knock out / put to sleep
