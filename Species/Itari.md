## Abilities
### Strengths
- Can produce mushroom spores that float out in all directions.
- [[Magic]] can be bounced between the spores if the Itari casts it. Magic bounced in this way cannot harm the Itari
- Spores will poison those that touch them

### Weaknesses
- Takes 50% more damage from [[Fire]], [[Ice]], and [[Light]]
- Spore production takes stamina
- Cannot use fire or light-based gear
