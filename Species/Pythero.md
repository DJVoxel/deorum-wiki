## Abilities
- Can move omnidirectionally
- Starts with 100 fire and learns it 20% faster
- Touch casting of fire-based magic is at double strength, and is 20% more efficient. While overheating, touch casting increases proportionally to their overheating negatives
- Learns a physical stat 10% faster
- Overheating (via movement) causes a decrease in max magical output, magical efficiency, blood and sensory stats.
- Non-touch casting methods are 20% less efficient.
- Takes 50% additional damage from weathering effects when at high-heat, dropping heat too quickly can shut down organs/jets.
- Learns Ice & water 50% slower.
## Appearance
- Has human-like skin of a chosen color that is connected by interlocking seams, their hair can be any color with a glossy finish. All over their body they have small concealed jets, with larger jets on their palms, elbows, feet, and both sides of their head in the form of ears that are elongated, angular, and project outward resembling fin-like structures. Parts progressively glow with ongoing usage. Has a retractable visor of your chosen color, and an antenna. Jets, eyes, and the inside of their mouth glow based on your chosen color.
