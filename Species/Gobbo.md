## Abilities
### Strengths
- Understands body language better
- Better at harvesting
- Can improvise skills at the cost of Stamina
- Every year, gains +5% learn rate in a [[Stats#Physical Stats|physical stat]] of choice

### Weaknesses
- Takes 33% more damage from Sensory effects
- Takes 20% more damage from the magic opposing its chosen magic
- Weapon and armour stat requirements are increased by 25%
- Hunted in some parts of the world
