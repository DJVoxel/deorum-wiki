## Abilities
### Strengths
- Immune to [[Charm]] and Poisons
- When injured, can go into an "Enraged" state where [[Dark]] magic increases by 50% and [[Summoning]] increases by 100%
- Can be broken into bits and pieces and not die

### Weaknesses
- There is a flap on the back of their head that when opened/hit, disables their magic use. The length of time that their magic is disabled for depends on the amount of force used
- Takes 15% more damage from [[Telekinesis]]
