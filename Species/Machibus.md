## Abilities
- During combat lets off a pheromone which will slowly impact the minds of enemies and allies, decreasing enemy's judgement and slowly charms them. Allies will have a passive buff to sensory stats and regeneration/recovery rate. The range of this and effects increase/change with age/combat experience
- Physical stats will increase the longer combat goes on for that fight
- Learns physicals 33% faster
- Wings are 66% resistant to damage
- Learns charm 25% faster. Learns all other magics 75% slower
- Can heal with dark
- Charm starts at 25
- Learns exterior skills 25% slower
- Often desires combat, will be impacted negatively without it
- Agility starts at 5
- Weak to light by 50%
- Long-term affecting magics inflicted by an enemy are 25% more effective

## Appearance
- 3.5 metres tall on average, skin can be red, orange, black, grey or white (or anything in between), has 4 horns that stem out from temples on head and top of head. Has 2 tough wings on back that run from ankles to just above head in length. Too heavy to fly.

## Trivia
- The first Machibus emerged from the depths of the underworld, likely a mutated variation of a charm-demon or succubus changed by the environment.
- A Machibus will usually use their sheer size and weight in physical combat to overpower their opponents and stay close to enemies in order to debuff them and slowly charm them, as well as increase their physical stats. Allies will also try to stay near the Machibus for buffs on themselves.
- Machubi’s heat season lasts 4 weeks, in which they will usually try to fight their desired person, although will not try anything past that, and usually only ask for sexual consent. If they like no-one in particular, or get rejected, they will take out their frustrations as violence in battle/fighting grounds.
- They are dimensionless
