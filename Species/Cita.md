## Appearance
- Crab claws
- Shell armor on legs and arms
## Personality

## Abilities
### Strengths
- Can breathe underwater
- Has crab claws that have 100 Grip Strength (separate stat that is additive with [[Stats#Bodily Stats|Strength]] when gripping)
- Has 50 [[Stats#Bodily Stats|Strength]]
- Learns [[Stats#Bodily Stats|Strength]] 100% Faster
- Has 30 [[Stats#Bodily Stats|Agility and Reflexes]], learning one of choice 25% faster and the other 10% faster
- Has shell armor on legs and arms that has a 25% resistance to Physical, [[Water]], [[Ice]], [[Fire]], [[Earth]], and [[Gravity]] damage.
### Weaknesses
- Weak flesh underneath the shell armor takes 10% extra damage from all sources
- Non-shell parts of the body has a 50% weakness to [[Ice]] and [[Fire]] damage
- Cannot wear armor on shell armor locations
- [[Stats#Bodily Stats|Dexterity and Flexibility]] are learned 25% slower
- [[Ice]] and [[Fire]] magic are learned 50% slower
- All other [[Magic]] is learned 20% slower
