## Abilities
- Can move about both physical and magical stats freely 
- Learns all physical and magical stats 100% faster
- Cannot use weapons other than knives and knuckle dusters
- Learns exterior skills 100% slower
- Weak to all physical and magical by 40%

## Appearance
- Eyes are a pie-chart and there’s a different section/colour for each stat, depending on what has more than the rest of the bar's move. 1 eye is for magic, the other is for physical.

## Funfact
- Switchera used to be a rare extra of the race, Vertian, but now they’re their own race due to how many of them exist and how different their DNA has become.
- Switchera's only trait that stays the same in combat is that they will always try to get close when intending to use a weapon as the only weapons they can use are close range.
