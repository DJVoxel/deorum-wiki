Skills (also known as Exterior Skills) represent your character's knowledge and experience with specific techniques and areas of study. These often require specialized training and are multipliers for your [[Stats]] in specific situations. Skills are each represented by a single number.
## Combat Skills
Combat Skills represent your proficiency in various combat arts, usually involving a specific weapon, but can be even more specific such as individual martial arts. Unlike other skills and stats, Combat skills have an additional fraction representing your progress towards your next skill increase. (i.e. Spear - 14 \[28/45\] is a spear skill with a value of 14, but has partial progress towards its next increase)
## Non-Combat Skills
Non-Combat skills represent other skills your character has learned. Anything from sciences to languages to geography, history, or even trade skills like carpentry fall under this category.
## Item Upgrade Skills
Also known as E-skills or E-mods, due to all starting with the letter E, these skills are highly specialized skills that allow you to modify items further than their usual properties would indicate. Many of these are still unknown.
