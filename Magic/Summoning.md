# Summoning
[[Summoning]] | [[Summoning Combat Guide]] |[[List of Summoning Species]]

***

An Overworld container magic. Early (5-100) Summoning primarily enables the user to draw forth a humanoid skeleton that somewhat intelligently follows orders. At low levels (100+) more options open up, such as creating zombies which have much more physicality than a skeleton or a ghost which do far better with magic. Creation of non-humanoid intelligent summons opens up, being significantly cheaper and more specialized. Specialization is available from the beginning with some control over body shape. Summoning allows the creation of basic weapons and armour.

### Movement
Summons follow orders from their summoner, and as such may be sent with specific instructions to complete tasks. This enables the Summoner to effectively be in more places at once. Summons may also simply carry their summoner.
### Buffs & Debuffs
Unseen.
### Healing
Summoning healing is unseen, with the closest equivalent being temporary limbs. A summoner with missing parts may affix parts of a summon to themselves to approximate the use of a limb. Replacement organs may not work without the use of [[Nature]] or another more standard healing magic placed within Summoning.

## Known Spells
The most common usage in spellwriting and other forms of witchcraft are summoning rituals to bring forth very specific being. Quality beginner demonic summoning books may automatically convert any magic into Summoning, but writers will need the magic to create the spells in the first place. These rituals, involving special materials have been known to create beings optimized for digging, running, killing, e.t.c.

There are no specific known Summoning spells.

## Interactions with other magics

**Summoning With** - Summoning is a container magic, able to use any magic as a basis for new types of creation. For instance, combining with [[Earth]] will enable dirt or stone summons that have high durability and strength.

**Giving Too** - Summoning may contain another magic for giving to a summon to use, enabling a summon to cast that magic. As such with [[Ice]], a summoner could conjure a being capable of casting Ice magic.

**Creation** - Creation magic focuses on the aspect of summoning involving non-intelligent beings. Another container magic capable of working with other owned magics, Creation is also able to use the general environment to inform the nature of the creation. Mostly used for weapons and armour for your summons to utilize. Creation at extremely high levels can mimic equivalents to living, evolving, enchanted, (e.t.c) gear.
