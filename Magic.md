## Starter Magic
Starter Magic is available to most species from birth. Usually start at 5.
- [[Earth]]
- [[Electric]]
- [[Fire]]
- [[Ice]]
- [[Nature]]
- [[Water]]
- [[Wind]]
## Basic Magic
Basic Magic can be self-taught with no pre-requisites. Once trained, starts at 5.
- [[Dark]]
- [[Light]]
## Simple Magic
Simple Magic must be learned from a book or teacher, but has no other pre-requisites. Once trained, starts at 0.
- [[Charm]]
- [[Magic Essence]]
- [[Telekinesis]]

## Modifiers
These are versions of any magics that are pre-fixed with a modifier, providing modified benefits
- [[Peace]] - Often stronger or easier to use outside of combat
## Universal Techniques
These techniques are available to (almost) every type of magic, but manifest different depending on the type of magic.
- Weapon Enhancement
- Magic Movement (Effect Depends on Magic)
- Orbs
- Buffing
- Debuffing
- Healing
## Tips:
- Give Techniques Names. This allows you to quickly use them in combat without needing to describe the effect every single time.
