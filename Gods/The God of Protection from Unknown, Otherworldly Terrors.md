### Responsibilities

The God of Protection from Unknown, Otherworldly Terrors has the self-imposed duty of safeguarding the continued existence of reality. As the strongest being in the multiverse he is only limited by how thin he is stretched performing this duty, often creating systems and rules to handle parts of his role & only stepping in once it is absolutely necessary. The God of Protection from Unknown, Otherworldly Terrors rarely does anything unnecessary but has notably engaged in social behavior of one to two sentences a few times in the past.

As the God of the Fourth Wall, he has set up various rules and filters for anything passing back and forth between the veil including automatic censorship. Attempted circumcision of rulings may result in a smite, although most perpetrators understand the implications of their actions, so those with no desire for circumvention need not worry.
